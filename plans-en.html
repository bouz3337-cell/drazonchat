How translations are counted

Plan limits are counted in translations. One message translated into one language is 1 translation.

Text
1 per messageLong messages count 1 more for every 200 characters of Korean, Chinese or Japanese, or every 500 characters in other languages. Messages with nothing to translate aren't counted.
Right-click translation
1 per messageThe same as text. Viewing the original or another language isn't counted.
Text in images
Usually 4 per image3 for reading an image, plus the translation of the text read. Images without text aren't counted, and up to 4 images per message are read.
Translation on images
Usually 6 per image3 for reading, the translation of each block of text, and 2 for drawing. Changing the font isn't counted.
Voice messages Coming soon
1 per second of audioTranscribing and translating voice messages sent from the phone app is being prepared. When it opens, the translation of what was said is added, and silence isn't counted.
Who pays
Translations opened from the image icons or by right-click count against the person who pressed, not the person who posted.

Frequently asked questions

When do payments open?

We're in a test period, so we aren't taking payments and everyone is on the Free plan. Payments open once business registration and the payment provider are set up, and we'll announce the date in the support server news and on this page. Korea pays in won, everywhere else in US dollars.

How do I cancel?

Once payments open, you cancel on the plans page of this website, the same place you subscribe. Cancelling stops the next payment, and you keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. The plan management screen in the bot's settings panel also brings you here.

How do refunds work?

You can get a full refund of your first payment within 7 days. We also refund for service outages or when the service differs from what this page says. The rest is in the Refund policy.

Are images stored?

The bot doesn't store them. Images go to Anthropic to read the text, and only the result is shown to you. Nothing is sent until you press the button. The text read isn't stored either; only the translation follows the translation cache rule (30 days).

Who makes the translations?

AI does (Claude by Anthropic). The same AI reads text in images. Translations can be wrong, so check the original for anything important. Images with translations drawn on them are marked as AI translations.
