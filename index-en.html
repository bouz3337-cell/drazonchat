Chat in your own language on Discord

DrazonChat is a Discord bot that translates chat into the other person's language. It posts the messages of people who turned it on under their own name and profile picture, translated, and it translates text in images too.

See how to startSee plans

Translations are made by AI (Claude by Anthropic). They can be wrong, so check the original for anything important.

What it does

In translations

  1. 01

    Auto-translation

    Run /translate on once and your messages in that server are posted in the other language, under your own name and profile picture. The original stays under the translation in small text, and the button shows the full original.

    Translations1 per message

  2. 02

    Text in images

    Menus, game screens, memes: if an image has text, it's read and translated. Open it from the icon under the images of people who turned translation on, or with right-click translation. Only the person who asked sees the result, and images without text aren't counted.

    TranslationsUsually 4 per image

  3. 03

    Translation on the image

    Get a copy of the image with the original text erased and the translation drawn in its place, from the icon. Pick a font (basic sans, comic, handwriting or bold title) and change it at no cost. It works best on horizontal text such as screenshots, memes and game screens, and only the person who pressed sees the result.

    TranslationsUsually 6 per image

  4. 04

    Voice messages Coming soon

    Transcribing and translating voice messages sent from the phone app is being prepared. We'll announce the day it opens in the news.

    Translations1 per second, plus the translationA 10-second message is usually 11

  5. 05

    Four tones

    Slang, casual, polite or formal, chosen per server. Casual with friends, formal somewhere new. On the Free plan you get all four for the first 14 days, then the polite tone.

    TranslationsPaid: all four. Free: the first 14 days

  6. 06

    31 languages

    31 languages are translated. The bot's screens and command names appear in 30 Discord languages, so the other person sets it up in their own language.

    Translations31 translated, 30 for screens

How to start

  1. 1

    A server admin adds the bot to the server. Ask for the invite link in the support server.

  2. 2

    Anyone who wants to be translated runs /translate on in that server. The bot guesses your language and theirs and opens the settings panel. Change them in the dropdowns if they're off.

  3. 3

    Write as usual, and the translation appears under your name. Start on the Free plan and change plans on the plans page when you need more.

Privacy

Messages to translate and images go only to Anthropic (US). The bot keeps the original of a posted translation for 14 days and cached translations for 30 days, and it doesn't store images or the text read from them. Chat content never goes into logs, and /translate delete-my-data deletes your data at any time.
