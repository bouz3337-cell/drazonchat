1. What the service is

DrazonChat is a Discord bot that translates chat into the other person's language. For people who turn auto-translation on themselves, it deletes their original message and reposts the translation in the same channel under their name and picture (through a Discord webhook). Some translations, such as right-click translation, are shown only to you.

Translation is done through the API of an AI model provider (Anthropic). What we send and how long it is kept is in the privacy policy.

AI translation can be wrong. For anything important, check the original with the 🔎 button on the translated message.

DrazonChat is not made or endorsed by Discord.

2. Who can use it

  • Any Discord user who follows Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. If you're not old enough to use Discord, you can't use DrazonChat.
  • A minor needs a legal guardian's consent to buy a paid plan. If a minor paid without that consent, the minor or the guardian can cancel the contract as the law allows.

3. Plans and subscriptions

  • There is a Free plan and there are paid plans. Each plan's monthly translations, features and price are as shown on the plans page.
  • Paid plans are monthly subscriptions that renew on the same date every month until you cancel.
  • Before you pay, we show the plan, the amount, the billing period, that it renews automatically, and how to cancel and get a refund, and we ask you to agree to these terms and the refund policy. Outside Korea, prices exclude tax, and the tax for your country is shown before you confirm the payment. We don't add any other fees or pre-select a paid option.
  • After you pay, we email you the contract details (plan, amount, billing period, next billing date, how to cancel and get a refund).

4. Payment

How you pay depends on your billing country.

  • Korea: you pay in KRW through a Korean payment processor. The operator is the seller, and prices include VAT.
  • Outside Korea: you pay in USD through Paddle. Prices exclude tax; you see the final price, including tax, at checkout. Paddle doesn't accept Korean billing addresses.

For purchases outside Korea: Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.

The bot never sends payment links. Payments and plan changes happen only on the plans page of our website. Don't click payment links you get in a DM.

5. Price changes

We also tell you how to decline and how to cancel. If you don't agree, we either keep your current price or let you cancel without penalty.
  • The same applies if we reduce the translations or remove features of a paid plan you subscribe to.

6. Cancelling

  • You can cancel a paid subscription at any time, and cancelling is as easy as signing up: use the "Manage plan" screen in the bot or the plans page on our website. You don't need to contact support.
  • After you cancel, you won't be charged again. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for, then move to the Free plan.
  • Removing the bot from a server or turning auto-translation off does not cancel a subscription.
  • Getting money back for a period you already paid for is covered in section 7.

7. Refunds

Your right to withdraw is limited for the part you have already used, and you can try the Free plan first.

The rules and how to ask are in the refund policy. In short:

  • Within 7 days of your first payment: full refund, however much you used
  • You cancel within 7 days of a monthly renewal: refund of the unused part of that period
  • An outage, or the service wasn't as described: refund, with no time limit

These terms and the refund policy don't limit your consumer rights under the law, including the right of withdrawal under Korean law.

8. Free plan and tone trial

  • The Free plan needs no payment, and we don't ask for payment details.
  • The Free plan includes a trial period, starting from your first translation, in which you can use every tone. The plans page shows how long it is. When it ends, the Free plan goes back to its default tone.
  • The Free plan and the trial never turn into a paid plan automatically. A paid plan starts only when you pay for it yourself.
  • If we change the Free plan's translations or features, we announce it in advance in the support server's announcements channel.
