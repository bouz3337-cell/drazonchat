Cozirom terms of service (draft)

Effective date: the day payments open (we aren't taking payments during the current test period)
Operator: 전요한, contact: bouz3337@gmail.com, support: https://discord.gg/96Jw2CYTpd

1. What the service is

Cozirom is a Discord bot that translates chat into the other person's language. For people who turn auto-translation on themselves, it deletes their original message and reposts the translation in the same channel under their name and picture (through a Discord webhook). Some translations, such as right-click translation, are shown only to you.

Translation is done through the API of an AI model provider (Anthropic). What we send and how long it is kept is in the privacy policy.

AI translation can be wrong. For anything important, check the original with the 🔎 button on the translated message.

Cozirom is not made or endorsed by Discord.

2. Who can use it

  • Any Discord user who follows Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. If you're not old enough to use Discord, you can't use Cozirom.
  • A minor needs a legal guardian's consent to buy a paid plan. If a minor paid without that consent, the minor or the guardian can cancel the contract as the law allows.

3. Plans and subscriptions

  • There is a Free plan and there are paid plans. Each plan's monthly translations, features and price are as shown on the plans page.
  • Paid plans are monthly subscriptions that renew on the same date every month until you cancel.
  • Before you pay, we show the plan, the amount, the billing period, that it renews automatically, and how to cancel and get a refund, and we ask you to agree to these terms and the refund policy. Outside Korea, prices exclude tax, and the tax for your country is shown before you confirm the payment. We don't add any other fees or pre-select a paid option.
  • After you pay, we email you the contract details (plan, amount, billing period, next billing date, how to cancel and get a refund).

4. Payment

How you pay depends on your billing country.

  • Korea: you pay in KRW through a Korean payment processor. The operator is the seller, and prices include VAT.
  • Outside Korea: you pay in USD through Paddle. Prices exclude tax; you see the final price, including tax, at checkout. Paddle doesn't accept Korean billing addresses.

For purchases outside Korea: Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.

The bot never sends payment links. Payments and plan changes happen only on the plans page of our website. Don't click payment links you get in a DM.

5. Price changes

  • Before we raise the price of a plan you subscribe to, or turn something free into a paid subscription, we tell you at least 30 days ahead and ask for your consent. We also tell you how to decline and how to cancel. If you don't agree, we either keep your current price or let you cancel without penalty.
  • The same applies if we reduce the translations or remove features of a paid plan you subscribe to.

6. Cancelling

  • You can cancel a paid subscription at any time, and cancelling is as easy as signing up: use the "Manage plan" screen in the bot or the plans page on our website. You don't need to contact support.
  • After you cancel, you won't be charged again. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for, then move to the Free plan.
  • Removing the bot from a server or turning auto-translation off does not cancel a subscription.
  • Getting money back for a period you already paid for is covered in section 7.

7. Refunds

Your right to withdraw is limited for the part you have already used, and you can try the Free plan first.

The rules and how to ask are in the refund policy. In short:

  • Within 7 days of a payment (first payment or renewal): refund of the part of that period you haven't used (in full if you haven't used any)
  • After those 7 days: you can cancel, without a refund. You won't be charged again and you keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.
  • An outage caused by us: we extend your plan period (section 10)
  • The service wasn't provided as the plans page describes: withdrawal within the period set by law

These terms and the refund policy don't limit your consumer rights under the law, including the right of withdrawal under Korean law.

8. Free plan and tone trial

  • The Free plan needs no payment, and we don't ask for payment details.
  • The Free plan includes a trial period, starting from your first translation, in which you can use every tone. The plans page shows how long it is. When it ends, the Free plan goes back to its default tone.
  • To stop people from getting the free allowance again and again with new accounts, Discord accounts created only recently get fewer free translations for their first few days.
  • The Free plan and the trial never turn into a paid plan automatically. A paid plan starts only when you pay for it yourself.
  • If we change the Free plan's translations or features, we announce it in advance in the support server's announcements channel.

9. Acceptable use

Don't use Cozirom for:

  • content that breaks the law or infringes other people's rights (copyright, privacy, reputation and so on)
  • translating, or putting translations on images of, works you have no rights to (comics, webtoons, books, video subtitles and so on) in order to post or distribute them
  • having someone's voice or conversation transcribed or translated from a recording made without their consent
  • impersonating others, or using translation to get around a server's rules or word filters
  • harassment, threats, hate speech, scams or spam
  • getting around limits or rate limits with automation or multiple accounts, or calling the bot in bulk
  • collecting translations in bulk to resell them, or using them to build or train another translation service or AI model
  • disrupting the bot, trying to break its security, or reverse engineering it

Sexually explicit content is not translated, under the translation model's usage policy. If the model declines, the original is left as is and nothing is counted. Translation is off in age-restricted channels, with two exceptions: the chat of age-restricted voice channels where voice-channel reading can be used is auto-translated so it can be read aloud, and voice message transcription works only in age-restricted channels. Voice-channel reading and voice message transcription come with paid plans, and checkout confirms that you're 19 or older.

Your responsibilities:

  • You own your messages and are responsible for them. A translated message we post (including translations of images and voice messages) counts as the message of the user who wrote or posted the original, and that user is responsible for what the original says and its effect on others.
  • We don't review what users post in advance. By turning translation on, you agree that we process your messages as far as needed to provide the service (translating, reposting, showing the original).
  • Only post images and audio you have the right to post. Use images or voices of other people only with their consent.
  • A server admin who adds the bot is responsible for making sure that using its features in that server (including features the admin turns on, such as a server plan's language channels) fits the server's rules and Discord's terms, and for telling the server's members.
  • When we receive a report or learn that content breaks the law, we may delete that translated message or stop translating for that user. We cooperate with lawful requests from authorities as far as the law requires.

If you break these rules, we may restrict your use (section 12).

10. Changes and outages

  • The service runs on Discord, Anthropic and payment processors, so it can stop or change because of them. When we can, we announce maintenance and major changes in the support server in advance.
  • If translation has to pause because costs suddenly spike, Free plan users are paused first.
  • If an outage caused by us keeps you from using paid features, we extend your plan period accordingly, following Korea's consumer dispute resolution standards for internet content. If we can't, we refund that part under the refund policy.
  • If we shut the service down, we tell you at least 30 days ahead and refund the unused part of your paid period.

12. Suspension and ending the contract
