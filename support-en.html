Cozirom help and support

Cozirom is a Discord bot that translates chat into the other person's language. It only translates messages from people who turned it on themselves, and it translates text in images too.

Getting started

A server admin adds the bot from the support server. Then anyone who wants to be translated runs /translate on in that server. The bot guesses your language and theirs and opens the settings panel; change them in the dropdowns if they're off. Write as usual, and the translation appears under your name.

Common commands

CommandWhat it does
/translate onTurns on auto-translation of your messages in this server. The first time, it guesses your language and theirs and opens the settings panel
/translate setupSettings panel: pick your language, their language and the tone, and turn translation on or off
/translate offTurns auto-translation off
/translate usageShows the translations you used this month and how many are left
/translate delete-my-dataDeletes the data the bot stores about you
/translate helpShows the commands, the latest news, and links to the support server and the privacy policy
Right-click a message → Apps → TranslateRead someone else's message in your language (only you see it)
Right-click a message with images → Apps → TranslateShows [Translate text] and [Put on image]. Translate the text in the images, or get a copy with the original text erased and the translation drawn in its place (only you see it)
Icons under the images of someone with auto-translation onThe page icon translates the text, the picture icon puts the translation on the image. Anyone can press them, and the result and the count belong to the person who pressed
Font dropdown under an image with the translation on itPick Basic sans, Comic, Handwriting or Bold title, and it's redrawn at no cost
Right-click a message → Apps → Delete my translationDeletes a translated message of yours

If your Discord is set to another language, the command names appear in that language.

Putting the translation on an image works best on screenshots, memes, game screens, signs and menus. Vertical text is drawn vertically (Latin letters, as in English, are turned to run along the column). Thai and Hindi can't be put on images yet.

Updates and plans are posted in the news and plans channels of the support server.

If a translation looks wrong, send a screenshot of the message and tell us which two languages it was between. Chat content is not kept in the bot's logs, so your screenshot helps the most.

Delete your data

  • In Discord, type /translate delete-my-data and press the confirm button within 10 minutes. It is deleted right away.
  • A translated message in a channel can be deleted at any time: right-click it → Apps → "Delete my translation".
  • If you can't do it yourself, ask through support above and the operator will delete it.
