DrazonChat help and support

DrazonChat is a Discord bot that translates chat into the other person's language. It only translates messages from people who turned it on themselves.

Common commands

CommandWhat it does
/translate onTurns on auto-translation of your messages in this server. The first time, it guesses your language and theirs and opens the settings panel
/translate setupSettings panel: pick your language, their language and the tone, and turn translation on or off
/translate offTurns auto-translation off
/translate usageShows the translations you used this month and how many are left
/translate delete-my-dataDeletes the data the bot stores about you
/translate helpShows the commands and a link to this page
Right-click a message → Apps → TranslateRead someone else's message in your language (only you see it)
Right-click a message → Apps → Delete my translationDeletes a translated message of yours

If your Discord is set to another language, the command names appear in that language.

Contact and problem reports

  • Email: bouz3337@gmail.com

Updates and plans are posted in the #업데이트-소식 (announcements) and #요금제 (plans) channels on the support server.

If a translation looks wrong, send a screenshot of the message and tell us which two languages it was between. Chat content is not kept in the bot's logs, so your screenshot helps the most.

Delete your data

  • In Discord, type /translate delete-my-data and press the confirm button within 10 minutes. It is deleted right away.
  • A translated message in a channel can be deleted at any time: right-click it → Apps → "Delete my translation".
  • If you can't do it yourself, ask through support above and the operator will delete it.

Plans

The bot is in a test period and doesn't take payments yet. Once payments open, you can change or cancel your plan on the plans page of this website. The bot never sends payment links.

Privacy

What is stored, for how long, and where messages are sent for translation is in the privacy policy.
