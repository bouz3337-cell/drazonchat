DrazonChat privacy policy

Last revised: 2026-10-20 (changes in section 9)
Effective date: 2026-10-15

1. What the service is

DrazonChat is a Discord bot that translates chat into the other person's language. It translates only the messages of people who turned translation on themselves, and it stops as soon as they turn it off.

3. What is sent out for translation

Translation is done with the Claude API of Anthropic PBC (United States). Voice message transcription is done with the ElevenLabs API of Eleven Labs Inc. (United States). We send:

  • Auto-translation: the message text, the writer's Discord display name (and username if different), and up to 6 recent messages from the same channel within the last 15 minutes written by users who turned auto-translation on (3 on the Free plan, 600 characters in total) with their display names. If the message contains words or names from your glossary, those entries (both cells) are sent too. The "My name" lines (display name and chosen spelling) of people who use the bot in that server are sent too, only when that name appears in the message.
  • Right-click translation and /translate once: the message you picked (or typed), its writer's name, and the same-channel context before it (same rule as above). The glossary used is the writer's entries and name line (same rule as above, including the name line of the person who pressed).
  • Text in images: if you press [Translate text] on the right-click translation screen or on the image icons of a translated message, the images in that message (up to 4, copies shrunk to 1,568 px on the long side) are sent to read their text, then the text read and its writer's name are sent for translation. If you press [Put on image] on the right-click translation screen, on the image icons or under the text translation result, the bot finds where the text is, sends a small copy of the image together with the text areas cut out of it in one request to read the text, and translates it (for an image with a lot of text you see the cost first, and once you confirm, the cut-out text areas are sent as one sheet per 16 text blocks, up to 3; finding, erasing and drawing happen on the bot's computer, and the image with the translation is shown only to you). The bot doesn't store the images or the text read (what it briefly keeps in memory for redrawing is in section 2; translations follow the translation cache rule). Nothing is sent until you press it.
  • Voice messages: if you press [Transcribe and translate] on the right-click translation screen, the audio file of that voice message (up to 3 minutes) is sent to ElevenLabs to be transcribed, then the transcript and its writer's name are sent to Anthropic for translation. The bot doesn't store the audio or the transcript (translations follow the translation cache rule). Nothing is sent until you press it.
  • Meaning explanations: the text of the message you picked (for a message DrazonChat translated and posted, the stored original you can see with the 🔎 button), its writer's name, and the same-channel context before it (same rule as above). The explanation is not stored and only you see it. How many you used each month is kept with your plan records (section 2).
  • Voice-channel reading (a feature being tested): in the operator's private test server, only when a consenting adult tester has picked a voice themselves, that tester's translations are sent to the Gemini API of Google LLC (United States) to be read aloud in the voice they picked. The screen where you pick a voice names where it is sent. Nobody else's messages are sent. It reads only in age-restricted voice channels and sends only the messages of people on a paid plan (messages of people on the free plan are not sent). The text read and the audio are not stored.
  • Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data is not used to train models and is deleted within 30 days (up to 2 years if flagged for a usage-policy violation).
  • Our ElevenLabs account is set so that the data we send is not used to train models. ElevenLabs may keep the audio and transcript it receives for the period set in its own privacy policy (we cannot shorten it).

Not sent by auto-translation: messages starting with //, messages with stickers or polls, messages over 1,500 characters, messages without letters. Text translated from the phrasebook or the cache is not sent for that translation (it may still be sent as context for a later message). Messages in age-restricted channels are not translated. The only exception is the chat of age-restricted voice channels where voice-channel reading can be used (section 7).

Requests from other people: anyone who can see your message in a channel can press right-click translation, meaning explanation, image text translation or voice message translation on it. The message (its first 1,500 characters if longer, its images, or the voice message audio) is then sent as described above, regardless of your translation settings. The // prefix applies only to auto-translation.

Cross-border transfer: items (list above), country (United States), recipient (Anthropic PBC, privacy@anthropic.com), purpose (translation, reading text in images, and meaning explanations), retention (30 days, with the exception above), how to refuse (turn translation off or don't turn it on; then nothing is sent. Requests pressed by other people work as described in "Requests from other people" above. Leave the second cell of your name line empty in the glossary tab and your chosen spelling isn't sent).

Voice messages: item (the audio file of the voice message), country (United States), recipient (Eleven Labs Inc., dpo@elevenlabs.io), purpose (transcription), retention (the period set in ElevenLabs' privacy policy), how to refuse (don't press [Transcribe and translate]; then nothing is sent).

Voice-channel reading (being tested): item (the testers' translations), country (United States), recipient (Google LLC), purpose (making the voice), retention (the period set in Google's Gemini API terms), how to refuse (don't agree to the test or don't pick a voice; then nothing is sent).

Nothing is sent to third parties other than Anthropic, ElevenLabs (only when a voice message is transcribed) and Google (only testers' translations in the voice-channel reading test). Discord's own processing is covered by Discord's privacy policy.

4. What is public in the channel

  • Translations are posted in the channel under the writer's name and picture. They are not removed by a deletion request; the writer can remove them at any time with message right-click → Apps → "Delete my translation". This works even if the bot's webhook has been deleted from the server: the bot then deletes its own message directly. To recognize the writer, the 🔎 button on a translated message carries the writer's Discord user ID. It isn't shown on screen, but it is part of the message data, so Discord apps and other bots that can read the channel can see it.
  • By default, the original is posted in small text under each translation (only the beginning of long messages). It is part of the translated message, so anyone in the channel can see it as long as that message exists, regardless of the 14-day retention in section 2 or a deletion request. You can turn it off per server in settings (/translate setup); it then applies to translations posted after that. As above, the writer can delete those translated messages at any time with "Delete my translation".
  • The 🔎 button on a translated message lets anyone in the channel see the full original and its writer for 14 days (shown only to the person who pressed it).
  • With auto-translation on, a message with only images and no text is also posted again under the writer's name and picture (the images are not translated and stay as they are). Messages with images get image icons that anyone in the channel can press to see a translation of the text in the images (shown only to the person who pressed it, and counted against that person's translations).

5. Logs

Operating logs keep only user IDs, counts, and the type and location of errors. No conversation content. When a deletion is processed, the user ID and the number of deleted rows are logged.

6. Deleting your data

  • In Discord, run /translate delete-my-data and press the confirm button within 10 minutes. It deletes everything in the table in section 2 except the translation cache, plus the profile picture and the recent messages and notice records in memory. If you use the bot again afterwards, new records start from then.
  • Translated messages posted in channels (including the original shown under them and the profile picture on them) are not deleted by a deletion request. The writer can delete them at any time with message right-click → Apps → "Delete my translation" (this works even if the bot's webhook was deleted; see section 4). The translation cache isn't linked to who sent the text, so it can't be picked out; it is deleted after 30 days.
  • Or ask through support (https://discord.gg/Q5yyQab9AM) and the operator will delete it.

7. Content we don't translate

Sexually explicit content is not translated, under the translation model's usage policy. If the model declines, the original is left as is and nothing is counted. Translation is off in age-restricted channels, with one exception: the chat of age-restricted voice channels where voice-channel reading can be used is auto-translated so it can be read aloud. Sexually explicit content is not translated or read there either. Age-restricted text channels and their threads, and right-click, image and voice message translation in those voice channels, stay off.

8. Your rights, deletion and safety measures

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop its processing. Deletion works right away as described in section 6; other requests go to support (https://discord.gg/Q5yyQab9AM) or email (bouz3337@gmail.com). To stop processing, turn auto-translation off with /translate off.
  • Legal basis: we process data to provide the translation feature you turned on yourself (performance of the service contract).
  • Deletion: when a retention period ends or we receive a deletion request, the data is deleted from the database and overwritten so that it doesn't stay in the database file. Things kept only in memory are removed after the periods in section 2 and disappear when the bot stops.
  • Safety measures: the database and the keys (API keys, bot token) are kept where only the operator can use them, and data is exchanged with outside services only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Logs don't keep conversation content (section 5).
