2. Within 7 days of a payment

  • For a first payment or a renewal, if you ask within 7 days of paying, you get back the share of that period's limit you didn't use. For example, if you used 10% of the limit, you get 90% of the payment back; if you used none, you get it all back.
  • If the contract details reached you later than the payment, the 7 days start on the day you received them.
  • Your right to withdraw is limited for the part you have already used. We say so on the plans screen and the checkout page before you pay, and you can try the Free plan first (Korean e-commerce act Article 17(2)(5) and 17(6)).
  • You ask on the refund request page of our website (https://pay.cozirom.com/refund): sign in with Discord to confirm it's you, check the amount you'll get back, and submit; it's processed right away. [Cancel plan] on the bot's plan management screen also links to this page. After a refund, your plan switches to Free right away.

3. Cancelling after 7 days

  • When you cancel, you won't be charged again. You keep your current plan until the end of the period you already paid for, and the rest of that period isn't refunded.
  • Sections 4 and 5, and cases where the law gives you more (section 8), are the exceptions.

4. Outages caused by us

  • If you couldn't use paid features because of a problem on our side, we extend your plan period accordingly, following Korea's consumer dispute resolution standards for internet content.
  • If we can't extend it, or you cancel before using the extra time, we refund the part we couldn't make up.
  • Outages of services we don't control, such as Discord, Anthropic or the payment processors, aren't caused by us (terms section 11). If one goes on for long, please tell us through support anyway.

5. Service not as described

If the service wasn't provided as the plans page describes, you can withdraw within 3 months of receiving it, and within 30 days of the day you learned or could have learned of it (Korean e-commerce act Article 17(3)).

6. How to ask

  • Ask right away on the bot's plan management screen, or write to us on the support server (https://discord.gg/96Jw2CYTpd) or by email (bouz3337@gmail.com) with your Discord username, the payment date and the order number from your payment confirmation email.
  • If you paid through Paddle (outside Korea), Paddle handles the refund as the Merchant of Record. You can ask Paddle directly using the instructions in Paddle's confirmation email, or ask us and we'll pass it on to Paddle.

7. When and how you get the money

  • For KRW payments, once a refund is approved, we cancel the payment or send the money within 3 business days. Paddle refunds follow Paddle's processing times. Your card issuer or bank may take a few more days.
  • Refunds go back to the payment method you used (card payments are cancelled). If that isn't possible, we tell you the alternative.
  • We don't deduct fees from refunds.

8. Your legal rights

  • This policy doesn't limit your consumer rights under the law, including the right of withdrawal under Korea's Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce. Where the law gives you more, the law applies.
  • If a minor paid without a legal guardian's consent, the minor or the guardian can cancel the contract as the law allows.
  • If you live outside Korea, you keep the consumer rights the law of your country gives you. If you paid through Paddle, Paddle's buyer terms apply as well.
